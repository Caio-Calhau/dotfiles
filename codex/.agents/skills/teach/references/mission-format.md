# Mission format

Create `MISSION.md` at the workspace root:

```md
# Mission: {Topic}

## Why
{The concrete real-world outcome driving this learning.}

## Success looks like
- {Specific observable capability}

## Constraints
- {Time, budget, prior commitments, or learning preferences}

## Out of scope
- {Adjacent topics intentionally excluded}
```

Keep one concrete mission per workspace and keep it shorter than one screen. Interview the user when the underlying outcome is vague. Revise it when reality changes, with confirmation.
